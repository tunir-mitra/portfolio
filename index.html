Behind the Resume

I build products at the intersection of people, technology, and business impact. My path into product started in engineering and accelerated at Adobe, where I spent three years leading digital transformations for Fortune 500 clients like Amazon, T-Mobile, and H&M. I shipped AI-powered features, ran agile sprints, wrote PRDs, and conducted 60+ proof-of-concepts across NA, EMEA, and APAC.

I approach every product the same way: start with the user problem, align stakeholders around clear priorities, and iterate fast. What motivates me is finding clarity in ambiguity and shipping things that actually move the needle.

I'm most passionate about 0-to-1 AI product development and go-to-market strategy for technically complex products.
