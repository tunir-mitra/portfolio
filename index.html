Behind the Resume

I build products at the intersection of people, technology, and business impact. My path into product started in engineering and accelerated at Adobe, where I spent three years leading digital transformations for Fortune 500 clients like Amazon, T-Mobile, and H&M. I shipped AI-powered features, ran agile sprints, wrote PRDs, and conducted 60+ proof-of-concepts across NA, EMEA, and APAC.

I approach every product the same way: start with the user problem, align stakeholders around clear priorities, and iterate fast. What motivates me is finding clarity in ambiguity and shipping things that actually move the needle.

I'm most passionate about 0-to-1 AI product development and go-to-market strategy for technically complex products.

  1. I'm leading the pilot of agentic AI tools like PM Lite and NebulaONE across 10+ healthcare projects, cutting setup time by 20% with 100% adoption. I've also redesigned intake and ticket workflows in Power Automate, reducing reporting effort by 40% and saving teams more than 15 hours every single week across projects.

  2. As a research and teaching assistant, I support the course Building and Applying Large Language Models at the University of Washington Information School. Reviewing how students approach LLM problems has made me much better at explaining technical ideas simply and spotting exactly where people tend to get stuck along the way.

  3. Most of it was at Adobe, where I led the product strategy and 0-to-1 launch of ACS Copilot, an internal GenAI tool that reached 85% adoption in 14 days while improving model accuracy by 15%. Before that, I built an AI metadata tagging system that classified 200TB+ of assets and cut content migration effort by 30% across global brands.

  4. During Covid, I co-founded Uncut Diamonds, a 0-to-1 upskilling and freelance marketplace for people who had lost their jobs during the pandemic. I owned the product vision, roadmap, and go-to-market, onboarding 330+ job seekers in our first 45 days and using GA4 to find and fix the exact points where they dropped off.
